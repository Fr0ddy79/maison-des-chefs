## Part 1: Target Diner Persona

### Primary Persona: "The Montreal Host"

**Demographics:**
- Age: 32–55
- Household income: $120K+
- Location: Montreal (central boroughs: Plateau, Mile End, Ville-Marie, West Island)
- Home ownership or long-term rental (private chef events require home stability)
- Couples and small groups (2–6 guests) are the core use case; larger dinner parties (8–16) are seasonal/occasional

**Psychographics:**
- Culturally engaged foodies — reads food media, follows chefs on Instagram, dines out regularly
- Hosts dinner parties for friends, family, and business colleagues
- Views at-home dining as a status signal ("I had a private chef for my anniversary")
- Values novelty and experience over convenience
- Willing to pay a premium for quality and exclusivity
- Not looking for "catering" — they want a *chef experience*

**Behavioral:**
- Discovers new dining concepts through Instagram, food blogs, word of mouth, and local press
- Books fine dining 1–2 weeks in advance for special occasions; more spur-of-the-moment for casual gatherings
- Reads reviews before committing — Google, Instagram, Yelp
- Shares memorable dining experiences socially (photos, stories, recommendations)
- Often recommends by telling a story, not just giving a link

**Discovery channels (by priority):**
1. **Instagram** — primary visual discovery platform for food experiences
2. **Word of mouth / referral** — friend recommendation is trusted
3. **Local press / food blogs** — Montreal Gazette, Eater Montreal, Taste MTL
4. **Google search** — "private chef Montreal," "personal chef for dinner party"
5. **Wedding/event planning sites** — for larger gatherings

### Secondary Persona: "The Occasion Planner"

- Uses the platform for specific occasions: anniversaries, proposals, intimate birthday dinners
- Lower frequency, higher emotional stakes
- Likely to search Google specifically for "private chef for anniversary Montreal"
- Needs trust signals and social proof more than discovery

## Part 2: Primary Acquisition Channel Recommendation

### Recommendation: **Local Food Media + Instagram First, SEO Second**

**Rationale:**

The platform's addressable market is Montreal — geographically concentrated, food-culture-rich. Paid ads (Google, Meta) are viable but:
- No brand recognition means paid traffic will be cold and expensive
- Private chef is a niche, high-consideration purchase — not a impulse buy that responds well to direct response ads
- The brand positioning (quiet luxury, editorial) doesn't match typical ad creative

**Instead, pursue three channels in priority order:**

| Priority | Channel | Why | Effort | Timeline |
|----------|---------|-----|--------|----------|
| **1** | Local food media outreach | Build credible third-party validation before launch; reach food-engaged audience | Medium | 2–4 weeks to coverage |
| **2** | Instagram organic + micro-influencers | Visual platform matches product; foodies already there; low cost | Medium | Ongoing |
| **3** | SEO / content | Capture high-intent search traffic ("private chef Montreal") | High | 3–6 months to rank |

### Channel 1: Local Food Media Outreach (Launch Priority)

**Target outlets:**
- **Montreal Gazette** (food section) — highest authority local press
- **Eater Montreal** — owned by Vox Media, covers emerging dining trends
- **Taste MTL** — local food culture publication
- **The Main** (Montreal Mirror successor) — cultural coverage
- **Foodista** — international food news site with Montreal readership

**Pitch angle:** "A new platform connecting Montreal diners with vetted private chefs — the Airbnb Luxe of dining experiences — launching soon in your city."

**Why this works for launch:**

- Editorial placement builds awareness at zero media cost
- Food media loves novel dining concepts — private chef marketplace is genuinely newsworthy
- A single Montreal Gazette food section mention drives more qualified traffic than $500 in Google ads

**Outreach process:**
1. Draft a press release / media kit (one-pager with concept, photos, chef count, launch date)
2. Identify food editors/journalists on LinkedIn and Twitter
3. Send personalized outreach 4–6 weeks before launch

### Channel 3: SEO / Content (Long-Term Growth Engine)

**Target keywords:**

- "personal chef for dinner party Montreal" — specific use case

- "hire a chef for home event Montreal" — alternative phrasing

**Content plan (see Part 3):** Launch content pieces will naturally support SEO.

**Time to impact:** 3–6 months for meaningful organic traffic. Not a launch tactic — a sustained channel.

## Part 3: Launch Content Plan (3–5 Initial Pieces)

### Content Principle

Every piece of launch content should serve **dual purposes**: acquire new diners AND build SEO/social proof assets. Each piece should be:
- High quality (not thin content)
- Shareable (visual or emotionally compelling)
- Evergreen (not dated)

### Piece 1: Chef Spotlight Series (3 posts)

**Format:** Instagram carousel + blog post on site
**Content:** "Meet [Chef Name]: [Cuisine] Chef | [X] Years Experience | [Signature dish or cooking philosophy]"
**Targets:** Discovery + social proof + SEO (chef name + cuisine in content)
**CTA:** "Book [Chef Name] for your next dinner party →"
**Why:** Chef spotlights are the most authentic acquisition content — they showcase the supply side of the marketplace in a compelling, human way. They also give chefs something to share, extending organic reach.

**SEO value:** Each spotlight targets "private chef Montreal [cuisine]" keywords.

### Piece 2: "What It's Actually Like to Host a Private Chef Dinner Party" (Testimonial/Story)

**Format:** Blog post + Instagram Reel
**Content:** First-person diner story — the preparation, the evening, the experience, the reactions from guests. Real names/photos if possible.
**Targets:** Remove the "weirdness" barrier for first-time diners; show the experience is normal, accessible, and worth doing
**CTA:** "Book Your Private Chef Experience →"
**Why:** The biggest acquisition objection is uncertainty: "Is this weird? Is this for me?" A relatable story removes that friction.

### Piece 3: "5 Reasons to Hire a Private Chef for Your Next Dinner Party" (Listicle)

**Format:** Blog post + Instagram carousel
**Content:** Practical reasons (no cooking, no cleanup, impress guests, dietary customization, unique experience) with supporting chef quotes or testimonials
**Targets:** Search traffic for "dinner party ideas Montreal" and "hire chef for party"
**CTA:** "Browse Available Chefs →"
**Why:** Captures search traffic from people planning dinner parties who may not have considered a private chef. High-intent, mid-funnel audience.

### Piece 4: "How to Choose the Right Private Chef for Your Montreal Dinner Party" (Guide)

**Format:** Long-form blog post
**Content:** Cuisine types, budget ranges, group size considerations, dietary needs, what to expect — comprehensive buying guide
**Targets:** SEO for "private chef Montreal," "personal chef for dinner party"
**CTA:** "Browse Our Verified Chefs →"
**Why:** High-intent, bottom-of-funnel content for people actively considering booking. Also builds trust through expertise demonstration.

### Piece 5: Launch Announcement / Press Outreach Package

**Format:** Press release + media kit
**Content:** Platform launch, number of chefs, unique value proposition, founder quote, 3 chef spotlights, demo/launch offer
**Targets:** Local food media (Montreal Gazette, Eater Montreal, etc.)
**CTA:** N/A (awareness, not conversion)
**Why:** Unblocks Channel 1 (media outreach). Every piece of content in this list can be included in the media kit.

## Part 4: Acquisition Tracking Setup

### UTM Parameter Framework

All acquisition traffic must be tagged with UTM parameters for channel-level attribution.

**UTM structure:**
```
utm_source = [channel]         # required
utm_medium = [medium]           # required
utm_campaign = [campaign_name]  # required
utm_content = [content_variant] # optional (A/B tests)
utm_term = [keyword]             # optional (paid search)
```

**Channel → Medium mapping:**

| Channel | utm_source | utm_medium | Example |
|---------|------------|------------|---------|
| Google search | google | cpc | `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=private-chef-montreal` |
| Instagram | instagram | organic | `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=chef-spotlight-1` |
| Instagram | instagram | influencer | `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=influencer&utm_campaign=[creator-handle]` |
| Local press | [outlet-name] | editorial | `utm_source=montreal-gazette&utm_medium=editorial&utm_campaign=launch-feature` |
| Email newsletter | newsletter | email | `utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=waitlist-launch-announcement` |
| Direct | (direct) | (none) | N/A |
| Referral | referral | referral | `utm_source=[referrer-domain]&utm_medium=referral` |

### Analytics Events to Implement

**Acquisition touchpoints:**

| Event Name | Trigger | Properties |
|------------|---------|------------|
| `page_viewed` | Any page load | `page`, `referrer`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` |
| `cta_clicked` | Any CTA button click | `cta_name`, `cta_location`, `utm_source` |
| `waitlist_signup` | Waitlist form submitted | `email`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` |

**Conversion funnel events:**

| Event Name | Trigger | Properties |
|------------|---------|------------|
| `chefs_page_viewed` | `/chefs` loaded | `utm_source`, `utm_medium` |
| `chef_profile_viewed` | `/chefs/[id]` loaded | `chef_id`, `utm_source` |
| `booking_form_started` | `/book` loaded | `chef_id`, `utm_source` |
| `inquiry_submitted` | Inquiry POST succeeds | `chef_id`, `guest_count`, `utm_source` |

### Implementation Notes

- UTM parameters should be captured on first touch and stored in `sessionStorage` / a cookie so they're available at conversion time (inquiry submission)
- Analytics should fire on all pages regardless of UTM presence (direct traffic has no UTM)
- For A/B tests already running (Hero CTA — MAI-2383; Booking Form — MAI-2623), add `utm_content` variants to test tracking

### Where to Implement

**Files to modify:**
- `src/lib/analytics.ts` — Add UTM capture helper and update all event calls to include UTM properties
- `src/components/WaitlistCapture.tsx` — Pass UTM data through with form submission
- `src/app/book/BookPageContent.tsx` — Capture `utm_source` in booking session state and pass to inquiry API
