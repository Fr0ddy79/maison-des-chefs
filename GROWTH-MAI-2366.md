## Growth Recommendation

### Hero CTA Copy Test

**Problem:** The primary CTA reads "Find Your Chef" — it's descriptive but passive. A more action-oriented, benefit-driven phrase could lift click-through from the hero section.

**Hypothesis:** "Browse Available Chefs" signals immediacy and availability, which is more compelling for diners ready to book. "Find Your Chef" implies search/exploration, which may attract browsers rather than high-intent bookers.

**Expected Impact:** +5-15% increase in hero CTA click-through rate, driven by higher-intent traffic reaching the chef listing.

**Test Design:**
- **Control:** "Find Your Chef" (current)
- **Variant:** "Browse Available Chefs"
- **Metric:** CTR from hero section CTA (primary), bounce rate on /chefs (secondary)
- **Duration:** 7 days or 500 clicks minimum

**Change Required:**
In `src/app/page.tsx`, two instances of the CTA button text need updating.
